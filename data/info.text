Where the same letter was sent to more than one office (for example, the Department Secretary and a subagency Director), we count it as one letter. 

Contacts marked as coming from a committee or committee staff are generally dropped from the analysis. 

Where legislators that share a name with another legislator are generally identified using information such as their chamber, congress, state, or district. Where we are unable to determine the legislator from agency records, we submit additional FOIA requests for more information. When no further information is provided, we drop the observation from our data and thus slightly under-count legislators whose names are not unique to a congress.
